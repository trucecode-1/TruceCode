Website Design Checklist for Kochi Businesses

Target keywords: website design in Kochi, website development in Kerala, mobile-friendly website design.

For business owners in Kochi, a modern website is a sales asset, not just a brochure. The checklist below focuses on conversion, SEO readiness, and mobile usability to help you generate service inquiries.

1. Mobile-first layout

Most local search traffic is mobile. Make sure your hero section, CTAs, and contact options are visible without scrolling.

2. Local SEO structure

Include your service area (Kochi, Kerala) in headings and page metadata. Google recommends clear structure and helpful content for users. See Google Search Central.

3. Fast load times

Speed is a ranking and conversion factor. Optimize images, minimize scripts, and validate Core Web Vitals. Guidance: web.dev Core Web Vitals.

4. Clear service positioning

Explain what you do in simple language. Use one primary CTA (email or inquiry form) to reduce friction.

5. Trust signals

Add reviews, project highlights, certifications, and a clear about section. These elements improve conversions and E‑E‑A‑T.
